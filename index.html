01 About
REC JM / 2026

I turn raw footage into stories that breathe, slow, warm, and full of intention.

Hi, I'm Jahnvi. I'm a video editor at the start of my journey, currently shaping reels, podcasts, and videos across short and long formats while building a body of work I'm proud of.

My approach is patient. I sit with the footage, find the beat inside of it, and build cuts that let emotion land. Whether it's a quiet brand film or a fast-paced reel, I care about the small stuff: the breath between cuts, the texture of the grade, the song that arrives just a second too late on purpose.
